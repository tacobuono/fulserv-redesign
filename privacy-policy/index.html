Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026.

Draft for the firm's review before launch.

What we collect

When you use the contact form we receive what you enter: your name, email, phone, company and message. Our web host keeps standard server logs such as IP address and browser type.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, schedule a consultation and, if you become a client, to provide our services. We do not sell personal information.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run the site and our practice, and only as needed for that purpose, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Inquiries that do not become engagements are deleted after a reasonable period. Client records are kept as professional rules and tax law require.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to mbreslin@fulservgroup.com.
