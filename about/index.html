A small firm with Big Four training

Fulserv Group has served clients across the New York metropolitan area since 1996. We focus on three things: resolving tax disputes, planning taxes, and planning estates and successions.

Michael E. Breslin

CPA, MST, JD
Managing Partner

Michael Breslin leads Fulserv and a team of 10 professionals. He joined the firm in 1998 after a decade with Big Four accounting firms. As both a CPA and a tax attorney, with a master's in taxation, he brings the accountant's command of the numbers and the lawyer's command of the rules to every case.

He closely supervises his team and remains deeply involved in all final projects. The firm has grown through referrals from satisfied clients and recommendations from other professionals, and it intends to keep growing without losing the small-firm ethos and close client relationships it has kept since 1996.

How we work

Integrity

Honest answers, including the ones you'd rather not hear, and full transparency about what we're doing and why.

Confidentiality

Tax problems are private. We treat your records and your situation that way.

Objectivity

Advice based on analysis of your actual finances, not on what's easiest to sell.

Current expertise

Tax law changes every year. We keep up with the rules, the regulations and how the agencies apply them.

Accountability

We track progress, review the strategy and adjust it when circumstances change.

Long relationships

Many clients have been with us for decades. We work to keep it that way.
