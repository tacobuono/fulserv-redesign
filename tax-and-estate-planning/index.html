Tax and estate planning in New York

Planning costs less than resolution. We look at your income, investments, business and family together, and build a plan that lowers what you owe now and what your heirs owe later.

What we plan for

Tax planning

We fold tax into your whole financial picture: when income and deductions land, how your business and pay are structured, and which credits and exemptions you qualify for. The aim is more after-tax income and fewer surprises in April.

Compliance and filings

Accurate, on-time returns, with risk reviewed before anything is submitted, so you meet every obligation without penalties or audit flags.

Estate planning

We help you see your estate clearly, decide where it should go, and use trusts, gifts and life insurance to get it there tax-efficiently. We work alongside your estate planning professionals so every document matches the plan.

Business succession

Handing a company to the next generation or to a buyer takes years of preparation. We plan the transfer to limit the tax cost and keep the business steady through it.

Wealth preservation and charitable giving

Ways to protect assets for future generations and put charitable intentions to work while reducing tax.

CFO, accounting, payroll and bookkeeping

For owners who want the books, payroll and returns handled by the same team that plans for them.

How planning works

  1. Getting to know you

    Before any engagement, we learn your situation, goals and concerns, and agree on a scope of work together.

  2. Your finances in one place

    We review your balance sheet and cash flow with you, and gather the returns and documents the plan depends on.

  3. Building the plan

    We set priorities with you and weigh the tax cost and the risk in each choice.

  4. Weighing tradeoffs

    We compare alternative scenarios side by side and turn them into recommendations you can act on.
